**BGAS** -- Botanical Garden Accession System <br>
**IPEN** -- the International Plant Exchange Network <br>
**IUCN** -- the International Union for Conservation of Nature <br>
**CITES** -- the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora <br>
