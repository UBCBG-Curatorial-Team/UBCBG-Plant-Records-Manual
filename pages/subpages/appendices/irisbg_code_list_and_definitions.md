# IrisBG Code list and definitions 
### Provenance [Accession-Origin] 

|Provenance|Code|Definition|Notes|
|-|-|-|-|
|Wild|W|of wild origin||
|Garden|G|of garden origin, propagation of cultivated material||
|Propagation of garden wild origin (seed)|Z|Propagation from seed of garden wild origin collection||
|Unrecorded|U|unknown origin||

### Determination Level [Accession-Determination] 

|Det. Level|Code|Definiton|Notes|
|-|-|-|-|
|Not determined |0|No plant ID made|The name change can be a taxonomic change (e.g. synonym change, or fix typos or other scenarios)|
|Provided by source|1|The name is provided without determinator information when a new accession is created. |Requires future ID verification|
|Det. by other garden (non-UBC)|2|The accession material of the same collection is determined by other garden.|Usually determination is made by other gardens from their own plant material of the same collector’s number. Our own plant material needs to be verified in future. e.g. identification in the Database of Asian Plants in Cultivation (DAPC)|
|Det. by comparison |3|The determination is made by comparing to other accessions, or verified by the plant description without keying. ||
|Det. by a taxonomist |4|Usually involved using a dichotomous key in a flora/monograph as references||
|Det. by a taxonomic specialist|5|Determined by a specialist of the plant group||
|Det. by breeder|6||Likely for hybrids, cultivar selection, etc.|
