# Create New Accessions Scenarios

|Scenario|Examples|Notes|
|-|-|-|
|New material is received [most common] ||apply to most of the new accessions |
|Unlabelled/mis-labelled plant(s) found that can not be traced to an existing accession [occasionally] |2015-0139 Rhododendron sp. |The plants are usually found during inventory. The label is lost and can not be certain which accession it belongs to; sometimes a wrong label has been attached to the plant.  |
|Multiple existing accessions (usually of the same taxon) have grown together or have been confused [rare] |2019-0876 (from 2009-0192 and 2011-0115) |Create a new accession and list the accessions confused in `Accession Comments`. Change the items status of the confused items of the existing accessions to `“Reassigned to new accession”` and make a note directing to the new combined accession. |
|Accession created from an item or items from a previous accession (items turned out to be from multiple taxa) [rare] ||Material was received in bulk (e.g. seeds or multiple plants) and turned out to be consisted of more than 1 taxa; each taxon needs an accession. |
|Accession with sexually propagated offspring from another accession [occasionally] |2012-0468, 2010-0491|New accession must be created for open- or self-pollinated seeds collected from another accession |
|Accession created from an item or items from a previous accession (when 1 or some of the planting items from a previous accession are  designated as cultivar) [rare] |1986-0593 *Carpinus fangiana* 'Wharton's Choice' (previous belonging to 1986-0558) ||
|Accession with only herbarium items or without planting items [sometimes] |2001-0676 |Accession with herbarium items only are often from expeditions; used as information for trip planning and data analysis |
|Accession with hybrid seed mixtures [occasionally] |1996-0171 *Miscanthus* (Mixed Cvs.) 1989-0269, 2019-0840 *Agapanthus* (Headbourne Hybrids) ||
|Accession with a group of unknown plants [occasionally] |2016-0423 |Usually for accession of native trees that needs plant identification |
