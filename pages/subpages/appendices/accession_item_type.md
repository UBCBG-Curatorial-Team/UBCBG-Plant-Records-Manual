# Accession Item Type
<img src="../figures/accession_item_types.png">

|Item type|Code|Definition|Notes|
|-|-|-|-|
|Planting|P|Plant material received, propagated, planted for the plant collection (usually for longterm) |Item number starts from 1 (nursery items from 99, 89 and lower). Can be: <br> -- Nursery items (seed store, propagation items) <br> -- 1 individual (e.g. a tree, a shrub) <br> -- a group of plants within the same planting bed and managed as a unit (e.g. herbaceous plants, small shrubs, hedges, thickets)|
|Flora (On-Site Origin)|F|Spontaneous plants that grow and reproduce without human care or intent in the garden area |Item number starts from 1; can be native plants (e.g. native trees), introduced weeds |
|Herbarium|H|Herbarium specimens that are associated with the accession |Item number starts from 200; specimens/vouchers can be collected from the source material (i.e. field collection), from the cultivated plantings from the Garden collection (i.e. from cultivated planting items). These specimens can be stored in house, UBC Herbarium or other herbaria. See [Add as new Herbarium item in IrisBG](/UBCBG-Plant-Records-Manual/add-as-new-item-in-irisbg)|
|Naturalized (UBCBG origin) |Z|When a cultivated accession spreads and grows on its own |Item number starts from 1 |
|Stegnon (Not Cultivated) |S|Record naturally occurring mosses, fungi, lichens |Item number starts from 1 |
|Temporary Planting |T|Plantings intended for short-term display; usually annual plants or plants grown for a specific limited time |Item number starts from 1; e.g. seasonal plantings of Food Garden, flower displays grown for a single season in the Entrance Garden |
|Tracked External Distribution |X|Index Seminum items; living plants given to other gardens |Item number starts from 100; item location: SEED or 8 if in nursery |
