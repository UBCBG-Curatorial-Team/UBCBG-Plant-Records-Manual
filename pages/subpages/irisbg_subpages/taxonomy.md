# Taxonomy 
<img align="right" src="../figures/Taxonomy.png" alt="the taxonomy section of the left toolbar in IrisBG" width="300"/>

Taxonomy information is created and maintained by the Accessions Technician or other users with the required privileges. Check with the informatics manager for user privilege. 

The [Taxa](/UBCBG-Plant-Records-Manual/taxa/) section in IrisBG allows users to manage the nomenclature, descriptions, images, and references of any taxon.

The [Taxonomic Groups](/UBCBG-Plant-Records-Manual/taxonomic-groups/) section allows for the creation, alteration, or organization of higher-level classifications of each taxon, such as family.
